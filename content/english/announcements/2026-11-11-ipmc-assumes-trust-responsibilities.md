As of November 11, 2026, the IETF Intellectual Property Management Corporation (IPMC) has taken on all responsibilities of the former IETF Trust.

The IETF Trust has transferred its assets to the IPMC, including the IETF trademarks, its rights in IETF documents, domain names, and the IANA intellectual property. The IPMC now holds and licenses this intellectual property for the benefit of the IETF and the Internet community, as the Trust did from 2005.

The IPMC was incorporated in Delaware on December 1, 2022, as the successor to the IETF Trust, and is governed by five Directors selected by the IETF NomCom, the IESG, and the ISOC Board of Trustees.

What this means:

- Requests that previously went to the IETF Trust, including license requests, now go to the IPMC. The contact address is unchanged: [trustees@ietf.org](mailto:trustees@ietf.org).
- This site, [www.ietf-ipm.org](https://www.ietf-ipm.org/), is the home of the IPMC's documents, policies, and announcements.
- The [IETF Trust website](https://trustee.ietf.org) remains available as an archive of the Trust's founding documents, minutes, financial statements, and announcements.
- References to the IETF Trust in IETF documentation and processes are being updated to refer to the IPMC.

Questions can be sent to the Directors at [trustees@ietf.org](mailto:trustees@ietf.org).
