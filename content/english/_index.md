The IETF Intellectual Property Management Corporation (IPMC) is a non-profit corporation, incorporated in Delaware on December 1, 2022, as the successor to the IETF Trust. In 2026, the Trust transferred its assets to the IPMC, including the IETF trademarks, its rights in IETF documents, domain names, and the IANA intellectual property.

The IPMC acquires, holds, maintains, and licenses intellectual and other property used in the Internet standards process and its administration. It does so for the benefit of the IETF and Internet community as a whole. Five Directors, selected by the IETF NomCom, the IESG, and the ISOC Board of Trustees, manage the IPMC under its [bylaws](/administration/bylaws/).

As of late 2026, we are in the process of updating other IETF documentation and process to substitute IPMC for prior references to the IETF Trust.

## The IETF Trust archive

The [IETF Trust website](https://trustee.ietf.org) remains available as an archive. It holds the Trust's founding documents, minutes, financial statements, and announcements from 2005 to 2026.
