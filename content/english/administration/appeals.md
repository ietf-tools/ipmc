If a member of the IETF community questions whether a decision or action of the IETF IPMC has been undertaken in accordance with IETF BCPs or IPMC operational guidelines, or questions whether the IPMC has created and maintained appropriate guidelines, they may ask the IPMC for a formal review of the decision or action.

The request for review should be addressed to the Chair of the IPMC Board of Directors at [trustees@ietf.org](mailto:trustees@ietf.org) and should include a description of the decision or action to be reviewed, an explanation of how, in the requester’s opinion, the decision or action violates the BCPs or operational guidelines, and a suggestion for how the situation could be rectified. All requests for review shall be posted publicly, and the IPMC is expected to respond to these requests within a reasonable period, typically within 90 days. It is up to the IPMC to determine what type of review and response is required, based on the nature of the review request. Based on the results of the review, the IPMC may choose to overturn its own decision, to change its operational guidelines to prevent further misunderstandings, to take other action as appropriate, or just to publish the review result and take no other action.

If a member of the IETF community is not satisfied with the IPMC’s response to their review request, they may escalate the issue by appealing the decision or action to the IAB, using the appeals procedures outlined in [RFC 2026](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc2026). If they are not satisfied with the IAB response, they can escalate the issue to the ISOC Board of Trustees, as described in RFC 2026.
