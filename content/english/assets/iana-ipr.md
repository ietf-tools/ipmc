The IETF IPMC holds certain intellectual property (domain names and trademarks) associated with the Internet Assigned Numbers Authority (IANA), in the service of the affected Internet operational communities. The IETF Trust assumed stewardship of this intellectual property in September 2016 and transferred it to the IPMC in 2026. The Trust's stewardship was the result of a two-year process that started in March 2014, when the U.S. Commerce Department’s National Telecommunications and Information Administration (NTIA) announced its intent to transfer the stewardship of key Internet functions to the global multistakeholder community.

## Background

The [IANA transition proposal](https://www.ianacg.org/icg-files/documents/IANA-transition-proposal-final.pdf) included a requirement for certain IPR associated with IANA to be transferred from ICANN to an entity independent of the IANA functions operator.

After discussion within the operational communities and the IETF Trust, the IETF Trust indicated that it would be willing to hold the IANA IPR. Among other things, the IETF Trust was created for the purpose of acquiring, holding, maintaining and licensing certain intellectual property for the advancement of the science and technology associated with the Internet and related technology. The IETF Trust agreed to hold the IANA IPR in the service of the affected Internet operational communities.

Representatives from the names community, the numbers community, the protocol parameter community, the IETF Trust, and ICANN drafted a set of agreements to effectuate the transfer of the IANA IPR from ICANN to the IETF Trust and to govern the relationships amongst the parties with respect to the IPR after the transfer. More background is in the [IANA Stewardship Transition Coordination Group documents](http://www.ianacg.org/coordination-group/icg-documents/).

## Executed Agreements

These agreements were executed by the IETF Trust on 30 September 2016.

1. [IANA IPR Assignment Agreement](https://trustee.ietf.org/wp-content/uploads/Assignment-Agreement-2016-09-30-Executed.pdf) (PDF)  
   This agreement transfers the IPR from ICANN to the IETF Trust.

2. [IANA IPR Community Agreement](https://trustee.ietf.org/wp-content/uploads/Community-Agreement-2016-09-30-Executed.pdf) (PDF)  
   This agreement explains the rights and obligations of the IETF Trust and each operational community as regards the IPR.

3. IANA IPR License Agreements  
   These agreements allow the IANA functions operator to use the IPR. There is one agreement for each of the names, numbers, and protocol parameters IANA services. In each case the licensee is ICANN, with the ability to sublicense to PTI.

    a. [IANA Names Services](https://trustee.ietf.org/wp-content/uploads/License-Agreement-Names-2016-09-30-Executed.pdf) (PDF)  
    b. [IANA Numbers Services](https://trustee.ietf.org/wp-content/uploads/License-Agreement-Numbers-2016-09-30-Executed.pdf) (PDF)  
    c. [IANA Protocol Parameter Services](https://trustee.ietf.org/wp-content/uploads/License-Agreement-Protocol-2016-09-30-Executed.pdf) (PDF)
